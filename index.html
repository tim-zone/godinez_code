A program built for builders

The BS Computer Science program at North Negros College is designed around one idea: students should leave able to actually build things, not just describe how they're built. Coursework moves from fundamentals into studio-style project work, so every concept gets tested against something real.

  • Hands-on labs in programming, networks, and databases
  • On-the-job training with local IT and BPO partners
  • A mentored capstone project in the final year
  • Preparation for careers in software, systems, and data
